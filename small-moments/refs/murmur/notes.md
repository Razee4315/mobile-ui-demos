# Murmur — LLM thinking companion
Refs: sources.txt · motion strips: ../_motion/motion_b.jpg (rows 027, 034, 041)

## What the research showed
01 iridescent-orb-light — orb lives on a pale field; colour stays INSIDE a soft rim, so the screen stays calm.
02 bubble-orb-warm-rim — the best motion ref: a thin warm rim plus one slow internal light that drifts around the sphere (15 s loop). Borrow the restraint.
03 shape-shifting-blob — a light gradient that morphs shape when "speaking". Use it for typing and thinking states only, not idle.
04 pink-orb-soft — blush on white works without feeling childish when saturation stays under ~35%.
05 light-chat-home — greeting + 3 prompt chips + recent topics: the right information density for an AI home.
06 orb-with-prompts — prompts stacked under the orb read as things the orb is offering.
07 minimal-answer-card — answers as quiet cards with one serif headline.
08 warm-gradient-voice — warm apricot to peach, never purple-blue.

## Signature: "the bubble"
A translucent soap-bubble orb drawn on canvas: hairline iridescent rim, three soft lights drifting inside, a slow 4 s breath.
- Attention: the inner light leans toward your pointer or caret, and the bubble squashes very slightly toward it.
- Typing: every keystroke sends a wobble through the rim and a mote of light flows from the caret into the bubble.
- Password: the bubble frosts over (lights fade, rim softens). "Show password" opens a clear window: it peeks.
- Error: a small no-shake and a rose blush for 600 ms.
- Success: it inflates and glides to the home screen (shared element), with lights swirling faster as it thinks.
- Touch anywhere: a soft ring spreads from the finger and the bubble ripples.

## 5 rules
1. One living object per screen; everything else is paper and type.
2. Colour lives only inside the bubble. UI chrome is ink on warm paper.
3. Newsreader italic for the voice of the product, Albert Sans for UI.
4. Every input gets a visible reaction within 120 ms; nothing reacts without a cause.
5. Motion is slow-in and slow-out (breath, drift, fade-up); no bounces, no spins.
