# Inkling — frame-by-frame animation app for indie animators
Refs: sources.txt · motion strips: ../_motion/motion_b.jpg (row 038)

## What the research showed
01 line-boil-birds — hand-drawn loops boil (lines re-drawn each frame) at 12 fps. The tell of real animation.
02 hand-drawn-line-illus — one weight of graphite line, generous paper, no fills.
03 line-art-yellow-accent — a single highlighter accent behind line art looks editorial.
04 pencil-wireframes — paper texture plus pencil gives instant craft.
05 sharpie-icons — icons can be drawn too; keep one line weight.
06 onboarding-slider-beige — a calm beige base with one rounded control.
07 calm-light-onboarding — a lot of air around a tiny illustration.
08 single-line-figure — a character in one continuous line.

## Signature: "Doodle" + onion skin
A one-line pencil bean drawn in SVG. It draws itself on entry (stroke reveal) and boils at 12 fps (turbulence seed swap, animated on twos). Ghosts in onion-skin red (previous frame) and blue (next frame) trail its moves, which is how animation software shows motion.
- Typing: a pencil underline scribbles under the field, and Doodle's eyes follow the caret.
- Password: Doodle scribbles a blindfold over its eyes. Show password: it lifts one corner.
- Error: a red pencil circle is drawn around the field.
- Success: the page flips like a flipbook.
- Touch or drag anywhere on the paper: you draw a pencil stroke that fades away.
- Home: a live 12 fps walk cycle in the project card, plus a draggable frame timeline.

## 5 rules
1. Graphite on paper; colour only from onion red, onion blue and one highlighter.
2. Drawn things animate on twos (stepped timing); UI chrome uses smooth easing.
3. Outfit for UI, Caveat only for handwritten annotations (max one per screen).
4. Drawn elements appear by being drawn (stroke reveal), never by fading.
5. Leave half the paper empty.
