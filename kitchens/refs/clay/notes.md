## 5 design rules
1. Everything is puffy: minimum radius 20px, no hairlines, and no shape without both an inner highlight and an outer soft shadow.
2. Raised means interactive, sunken means input. Buttons pop out with a thick bottom lip; fields press in with an inner shadow.
3. Pastels sit on warm cream, never on white. Text is deep cocoa (#4A2E22), never black.
4. The mascot breaks the frame: hero objects overlap card edges and float with a slow 6–8 s bob.
5. Motion squishes: presses scale to 0.94 with a slight vertical squash, and screens transition with a jelly overshoot.
