## 5 design rules
1. Three layers always: deep-space backdrop, then floating glass/chrome objects, then frosted panels (backdrop blur 24px or more, 6–10% white fill).
2. Light lives on the edges: 1px gradient strokes (cyan to violet to transparent) and no solid borders.
3. Saturation is rationed: only the primary CTA and the focus state use the full cyan-to-magenta gradient; everything else is navy and white at varying opacity.
4. Sora for statements, JetBrains Mono for data (ETAs, prices, codes, timers), always uppercase and tracked for labels.
5. Motion blurs: screens cross-fade with blur(12px) and slight scale; nothing bounces.
