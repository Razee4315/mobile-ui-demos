## 5 design rules
1. Every element is 3px solid #111 outlined, flat filled and shadowed with a hard offset (no blur). Press means the shadow goes to 0 and the element translates by the same amount.
2. Archivo Black is used huge and tight (−2% tracking, often uppercase) and is allowed to crop or rotate; Space Grotesk handles everything readable.
3. Stickers are rotated a few degrees and overlap edges; nothing is perfectly centred twice in a row.
4. Colour blocks, not gradients: each section owns one flat colour from the palette; text is always black or off-white for AA contrast.
5. Motion is a hard cut plus bounce: the screen snaps in, then content pops with an overshoot spring; buttons "clunk" down on press.
