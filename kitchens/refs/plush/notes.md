## 5 design rules
1. Every container is a fabric patch: oatmeal felt fill (subtle noise texture), radius 18–26px, and a dashed "stitch" outline inset 5–7px.
2. Fraunces carries the warmth (soft, optical-size, slightly wonky italic for accents); DM Sans stays quiet for UI.
3. Shadows are short and warm (brown-tinted, never grey); objects look like they rest on a blanket rather than float high.
4. Terracotta is the action colour, sage means success and calm, and dusty rose is for gentle accents. No pure white anywhere.
5. Motion is soft and slow: a gentle settle (ease-out, 450ms) as if dropped onto a cushion; no snapping.
