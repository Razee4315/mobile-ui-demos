## 5 design rules
1. Air first: at least 40% of every screen is untouched white or pearl, with one hero object per screen.
2. Surfaces are pearl glass: white at 55–70% with blur, a 1px white inner highlight on top and a very soft lavender-tinted shadow.
3. Manrope only, using weight contrast (300 display vs 700 labels) and tight negative tracking on big type.
4. Iridescence is the accent: a conic aurora ring for focus, loaders and the active tab; the CTA is deep ink (#1B1E3C) with a chrome sheen sweep.
5. Motion is liquid: slow drift of blobs (12–20 s), page transitions slide up with a gentle blur-to-sharp, and a sheen sweeps across the CTA on hover.
